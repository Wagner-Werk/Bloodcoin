Stake BCUS

Not connected

Each week new BCUS are created. How many depends on the mortality factor reported by the oracle. Half of them go to public mining, the other half is offered to stakers here. Stakers receive their share of the circulating supply, at most 1% of their stake per week, paid out evenly over the following seven days. Whatever is above that goes to the miners. This is a technical limit, not a promise of any return.

How it works

I

Stake

You move BCUS into the staking contract. They stay yours and you can take them back at any time.

II

Weekly emission

Once a week the emission contract creates new BCUS. Half of them are offered to this pool; stakers get their share of the circulating supply, at most 1% of their stake per week, and the rest goes to the miners.

III

Over seven days

The new BCUS are paid out evenly over the next seven days, split by each staker's share of the pool.

IV

Nobody staked

Whatever arrives while nobody has anything staked is burned or goes to the miners. Nobody collects it later.

Before you stake

  • This is not an offer and not an investment. There is no promised or expected return. How much you receive depends on how much is emitted and how much everyone else has staked, and both change every week.
  • BCUS can lose all of their value. Rewards are paid in BCUS.
  • The contracts have no owner. Nobody can change or pause them, including us, so nobody can rescue funds after a mistake either.
  • The contracts have not been audited by an independent firm yet.
  • There is no lock-up and no fee. You can unstake at any time.
  • Not available to persons in the United States.
